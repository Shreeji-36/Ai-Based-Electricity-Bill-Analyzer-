# User Manual

1. Register or log in.
2. Choose your industry on the home page.
3. Upload your electricity bill (JPG, PNG or PDF). Check the extracted values and fix any errors.
4. Enter the average hours per day each machine runs. Leave "Others" alone; it is calculated for you.
5. Open the dashboard: machine-wise units and cost, charts, forecast, anomalies and recommendations.
6. Download the report as PDF, Excel or CSV.

Tip: the more monthly bills you add, the more accurate the forecast and anomaly detection become.
